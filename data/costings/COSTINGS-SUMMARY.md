## Cost stack (what is included)

| Layer | What’s in it |
|-------|----------------|
| Ingredients | Recipe amounts × Checkers pack unit cost |
| Wastage 5% | Spill, trim, tester bites |
| Packaging | Box R8.5 + liner R2 + label R1.5 + card R1 + tape R0.5 |
| Electricity | Oven 2.2 kW × hours × R3.5/kWh; batch of 10 uses 45% of solo kWh per box |
| Other | Cleaning R1.5 + equipment wear R2 |
| Labour | R0 in cash cost (owner time not charged) — add rate in script if you want |

## Pricing rule used

- Target ~52% gross margin on sell price after full cash cost  
- And at least 2.15× cost  
- Round up to nearest R5  
- **Primary recommended sell** uses **batch-of-10** cost (realistic pilot)
