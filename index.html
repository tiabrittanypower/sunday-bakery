Hello, I’m Tia

A bakery built around
the good kind of waiting.

Sunday started in a home kitchen, with one oven and a stubborn belief that weekend baking tastes better when nobody rushes it.

These days I bake weekly boxes for collection in Boksburg and Kempton Park. The menu rotates — classic tea table, chocolate treats, braai weekend, milk tart — and quantities stay small so every loaf, bun and square gets the attention it deserves.

Where do I collect my order?+

In the Boksburg / Kempton Park area. The exact collection point and map notes are on your order confirmation (home gate or partner point, depending on the week).

Can I walk in without ordering?+

No — we bake to order only so nothing is wasted. Pre-ordering by Friday is the only way to get a box.

What if I miss my pickup time?+

Don’t panic. Your order stays boxed until the end of the collection window. After that, send us a message and we’ll do our best to help.

Do you cater for allergies?+

Our kitchen handles wheat, gluten, dairy, eggs, sesame and nuts. Ingredients are listed per product, but we cannot guarantee against traces.

Can I change or cancel my order?+

Reply to your confirmation before Thursday at 18:00 and we’ll happily help. After that, ingredients and prep are already underway.
