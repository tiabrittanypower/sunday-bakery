## Inside card — storage & reheat (generic shell)

```
Thank you for collecting your Sunday box.

HOW TO KEEP IT
• Loaves, brownies, cookies, biscuits: airtight, cool room. 2–3 days (biscuits up to 5).
• Buns, rolls, scones, muffins: loose wrap. Best in 2 days. Freeze OK.
• Malva, milk tart, ganache cupcakes: fridge. Eat in 2–3 days.

HOW TO WARM
• Lemon / vanilla / banana loaf: 160°C · 8–10 min (or microwave 15–20 s/slice).
• Cinnamon buns / rolls: 160°C · 5–8 min, covered loosely.
• Scones / muffins: 160°C · 5 min.
• Brownies: 10–15 s microwave for fudgy centre.
• Malva: 150°C · 10–12 min; pour sauce over.
• Milk tart squares: best cold from the fridge.

ALLERGENS THIS WEEK
[paste week-specific line]

Questions? Message us — we’re a one-baker kitchen and we care that you enjoy it.
Next Sunday’s theme drops Monday on WhatsApp.
```

## Ingredient summary lines (for full label)

### Week 1 — After-Church Classic
Lemon loaf (wheat flour, sugar, eggs, butter/oil, milk, lemon, raising agents); cinnamon buns (wheat flour, milk, butter, sugar, egg, yeast, cinnamon); jam shortbreads (wheat flour, butter, sugar, apricot jam); cheese scones (wheat flour, butter, cheddar, milk, chives).

### Week 2 — Chocolate Sunday
Brownies (butter, sugar, eggs, cocoa, wheat flour, chocolate); cookies (wheat flour, butter, sugar, egg, chocolate); cupcakes + ganache (wheat flour, sugar, egg, oil/butter, milk, cream, chocolate); cocoa banana loaf (banana, wheat flour, sugar, egg, oil, cocoa).

### Week 3 — Braai Weekend
Malva + sauce (wheat flour, sugar, egg, apricot jam, milk, butter, cream, raising agents, vinegar); corn muffins (wheat flour, sweetcorn, egg, milk, oil, cheddar); milk rolls (wheat flour, milk, butter, yeast, sugar); ginger biscuits (wheat flour, butter, sugar, ginger, syrup).

### Week 4 — Tea-Time Home
Scones (wheat flour, butter, milk, sugar, raisins); milk tart squares (milk/condensed milk, eggs, wheat flour, butter, sugar, cinnamon); vanilla loaf (wheat flour, sugar, eggs, butter/oil, milk, vanilla); shortbread (wheat flour, butter, sugar); jam (fruit, sugar).
