# Month 1 recipes — full methods

**Kitchen:** home oven · mixing bowls · sheet pans · loaf tin · muffin tin · rolling pin  
**Scale:** quantities = **1 customer box**. For 10 or 15 boxes, multiply every ingredient (and plan oven space).  
**Oven temps:** conventional; if fan-forced, reduce ~10–15 °C and watch early.

**Safety:** cool completely before packing. Fridge for malva, milk tart, ganache in hot weather.

# Week 1 — After-Church Classic (sell R245)

## 1. Lemon drizzle loaf (1 small loaf ≈ 8 slices)

**Ingredients**
- 200 g cake flour  
- 10 g baking powder  
- pinch salt  
- 150 g white sugar  
- 2 large eggs  
- 100 g butter, melted (or 80 g oil + 20 g butter)  
- 80 ml milk  
- Zest of 1–2 lemons + 30 ml lemon juice  
- **Drizzle:** 60 g icing or castor sugar + 20–25 ml lemon juice  

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 170 °C. Grease and line a small loaf tin.  
2. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt.  
3. Beat sugar, eggs, melted butter, milk, zest, juice until smooth.  
4. Fold in dry mix — do not overmix.  
5. Bake 40–50 min until skewer comes out clean.  
6. Cool 10 min in tin. Mix drizzle; poke loaf lightly; pour over while warm. Cool fully before wrap.

**Scale 10 boxes:** 2 kg flour, 1.5 kg sugar, 20 eggs, 1 kg butter, ~1 L milk, 15–20 lemons.

## 2. Soft cinnamon swirl buns (4 large)

**Dough**
- 300 g bread flour  
- 7 g instant yeast  
- 40 g sugar  
- 5 g salt  
- 180 ml warm milk  
- 40 g soft butter  
- 1 egg  

**Filling**
- 40 g soft butter  
- 50 g brown sugar  
- 2 tsp cinnamon  

**Glaze (optional)**
- 40 g icing sugar + splash milk  

**Method**
1. Mix dough ingredients; knead 8–10 min until smooth. Rise 45–60 min until doubled.  
2. Roll rectangle ~25×35 cm. Spread butter; mix sugar+cinnamon; sprinkle.  
3. Roll up long side; cut 4 equal buns. Place in greased dish.  
4. Cover; rise 30–40 min (or fridge overnight after shaping — bake from cool, add 10–15 min proof).  
5. Bake 180 °C · 18–22 min until golden. Cool on rack. Glaze when warm if using.

**Scale 10 boxes:** 3 kg bread flour, 10 sachets yeast, ~2 L milk, 10 eggs, 800 g butter total for dough+filling.

## 3. Apricot jam thumbprint shortbreads (8)

- 150 g cake flour  
- 100 g cold butter, cubed  
- 50 g castor sugar  
- pinch salt  
- ½ tsp vanilla  
- ~40 g apricot jam  

**Method**
1. Rub butter into flour+sugar+salt to crumbs; add vanilla; press dough together (do not overwork). Chill 30 min.  
2. Roll 8 balls; place on lined tray; thumb indent each.  
3. Fill indents with jam.  
4. Bake 170 °C · 12–15 min until edges pale gold. Cool on tray (they firm up).

## 4. Cheese & chive scones (4 medium)

- 200 g cake flour  
- 10 g baking powder  
- pinch salt + black pepper  
- 50 g cold butter  
- 80 g grated cheddar  
- 1 tbsp chopped chives (optional)  
- ~120–140 ml cold milk  

**Method**
1. Oven 200 °C. Flour+baking powder+salt; rub in butter.  
2. Stir cheese+chives; add milk to soft dough.  
3. Pat 2 cm thick; cut 4 scones. Brush milk.  
4. Bake 12–15 min until risen and golden. Cool on rack (loose wrap later).

# Week 2 — Chocolate Sunday (sell R265)

## 1. Fudgy brownies (9 squares)

- 150 g butter  
- 200 g sugar  
- 2 large eggs  
- 60 g cocoa, sifted  
- 80 g cake flour  
- pinch salt  
- 50–80 g chocolate chips (optional)  
- 1 tsp vanilla  

**Method**
1. Oven 170 °C. Line 20 cm square tin.  
2. Melt butter; whisk in sugar, then eggs + vanilla.  
3. Fold cocoa, flour, salt, chips.  
4. Bake 22–28 min — centre slightly soft. Cool fully (overnight = cleaner cuts). Cut 3×3.

---

## 2. Double-chocolate chip cookies (8 large)

- 150 g cake flour  
- ½ tsp bicarb  
- pinch salt  
- 100 g butter, soft  
- 80 g brown sugar + 40 g white sugar  
- 1 egg  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- 100 g chocolate chips/chunks  
- 1 tbsp cocoa (optional “double”)  

**Method**
1. Cream butter+sugars; beat in egg+vanilla.  
2. Mix flour, bicarb, salt, cocoa; combine; fold chips.  
3. Scoop 8 mounds; chill 20 min if soft.  
4. Bake 170 °C · 12–14 min until edges set, centres soft. Cool on tray.

---

## 3. Vanilla cupcakes + chocolate ganache (4)

**Cupcakes**
- 100 g cake flour  
- 80 g sugar  
- 1 tsp baking powder  
- 1 egg  
- 60 ml oil (or 50 g melted butter)  
- 60 ml milk  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- pinch salt  

**Ganache**
- 80 g dark/milk chocolate  
- 60–80 ml cream  

**Method**
1. Oven 170 °C. Line 4 muffin cups.  
2. Whisk dry; whisk wet; combine gently. Fill cups ⅔. Bake 15–18 min. Cool completely.  
3. Heat cream to just simmer; pour over chopped chocolate; rest 2 min; stir smooth. Cool until spreadable.  
4. Flat-top cupcakes with ganache (no tall peaks). Set in fridge 20 min before pack.

## 4. Cocoa banana loaf (1 small)

- 2–3 very ripe bananas, mashed (~250 g)  
- 80 g sugar  
- 80 ml oil  
- 1–2 eggs  
- 200 g cake flour  
- 20–30 g cocoa  
- 1 tsp bicarb  
- pinch salt  
- 1 tsp vanilla  

**Method**
1. Oven 170 °C. Line small loaf tin.  
2. Mix banana, sugar, oil, eggs, vanilla.  
3. Fold flour, cocoa, bicarb, salt.  
4. Bake 45–55 min until skewer clean. Cool fully.

# Week 3 — Braai Weekend Box (sell R265)

## 1. Malva pudding + sauce (~6 portions in foil tray)

**Pudding**
- 150 g sugar  
- 1 large egg  
- 1 tbsp apricot jam  
- 150 g cake flour  
- 1 tsp bicarb  
- pinch salt  
- 1 tbsp butter, melted  
- 1 tsp vinegar  
- 100 ml milk  

**Sauce**
- 100 ml cream  
- 80 g sugar  
- 50 g butter  
- 50 ml hot water or milk  
- ½ tsp vanilla  

**Method**
1. Oven 180 °C. Grease small baking dish.  
2. Beat sugar+egg; mix jam. Add flour+bicarb+salt alternately with milk, melted butter, vinegar.  
3. Bake 25–35 min until springy and brown.  
4. Simmer sauce ingredients until sugar dissolves.  
5. Pour half sauce over hot pudding; reserve half in jar for customer. Cool; portion into foil tray; fridge.

**Customer card:** reheat 150 °C · 10–12 min; pour remaining sauce over.

## 2. Cheddar corn muffins (6)

- 200 g cake flour  
- 10 g baking powder  
- pinch salt  
- 1 tbsp sugar  
- 1 egg  
- 150 ml milk  
- 60 ml oil  
- ½ tin sweetcorn, drained (~140 g)  
- 50 g grated cheddar (optional)  

**Method**
1. Oven 190 °C. Grease 6 muffin cups.  
2. Mix dry; mix wet; combine; fold corn+cheese.  
3. Bake 15–18 min until golden. Cool on rack.

## 3. Soft milk dinner rolls (6)

- 300 g bread flour  
- 7 g instant yeast  
- 25 g sugar  
- 5 g salt  
- 160–180 ml warm milk  
- 30 g soft butter  
- 1 egg (optional for richer dough — if used, reduce milk slightly)  

**Method**
1. Mix and knead until smooth; rise 45–60 min.  
2. Divide 6; shape balls; tray; rise 25–35 min.  
3. Bake 190 °C · 12–15 min until golden. Brush butter optional. Cool.

## 4. Ginger biscuits (10)

- 150 g cake flour  
- ½ tsp bicarb  
- 2 tsp ground ginger  
- 75 g butter  
- 75 g sugar  
- 1 tbsp golden syrup or honey  
- 1 egg yolk (or splash milk if skipping yolk)  

**Method**
1. Cream butter+sugar; mix syrup, yolk.  
2. Add flour, bicarb, ginger to firm dough. Chill 20 min.  
3. Roll 10 balls; flatten slightly.  
4. Bake 170 °C · 10–12 min. Cool on tray.

# Week 4 — Tea-Time Home Edition (sell R250)

## 1. Plain + fruit scones (6 = 3 + 3)

- 300 g cake flour  
- 15 g baking powder  
- pinch salt  
- 60 g cold butter  
- 40 g sugar  
- ~160–180 ml cold milk  
- 40 g raisins (for fruit half only)  

**Method**
1. Oven 200 °C. Rub butter into flour+baking powder+salt+sugar.  
2. Split dough idea: half plain milk; half milk+raisins — or make one dough, fold raisins into half.  
3. Pat 2 cm; cut 6. Brush milk. Bake 12–15 min. Cool; reheat note for customer.

---

## 2. Milk tart squares (9)

**Base**
- 120 g cake flour  
- 60 g butter  
- 30 g sugar  
- pinch salt  

**Custard (condensed-milk style)**
- 1 tin condensed milk (385 g) — *use ~half to two-thirds for 20 cm pan if too sweet; or full for richer*  
- 500 ml milk (adjust if using full tin — taste and thickness)  
- 2–3 eggs  
- 2 tbsp cake flour or maizena  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- cinnamon to dust  

*Simpler home method:*  
1. Base: rub butter into flour+sugar; press into lined 20 cm tin; bake 170 °C · 12–15 min pale gold.  
2. Whisk milk, condensed milk (start with ½ tin), eggs, flour, vanilla; pour over base.  
3. Bake 160 °C · 25–35 min until just set with slight wobble. Cool; fridge overnight. Dust cinnamon; cut 3×3 cold.

## 3. Vanilla butter loaf (1 small)

- 200 g cake flour  
- 10 g baking powder  
- 150 g sugar  
- 2 eggs  
- 100 g butter, soft (or 80 ml oil)  
- 80 ml milk  
- 2 tsp vanilla  
- pinch salt  

**Method**
1. Oven 170 °C. Cream butter+sugar; beat eggs; add vanilla.  
2. Alternate flour mix and milk.  
3. Bake 40–50 min. Cool fully.

---

## 4. Shortbread fingers (8)

- 150 g cake flour  
- 100 g butter  
- 50 g castor sugar  
- pinch salt  

**Method**
1. Cream butter+sugar; mix flour+salt to dough. Chill 20 min.  
2. Press or roll; cut 8 fingers; prick with fork.  
3. Bake 160 °C · 15–20 min pale (not brown). Cool on tray.

## Scaling cheat sheet

| Boxes | Multiply ingredients by | Oven strategy |
|-------|-------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | 1× | Single batches |
| 10 | 10× | Multiple trays; freeze cookie/shortbread dough ahead if needed |
| 15 | 15× | Two-day bake; do not skip cooling |

**Weigh flour** when scaling — volume cups drift badly at 15×.
